Privacy Policy

How HC Agency collects, protects, and handles your personal information with complete transparency.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

At HC Agency ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to safeguarding your privacy and protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our website at hcagency.tn, contact us, or engage our web design, development, and digital strategy services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways depending on your interaction with our services:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and project specifications provided when submitting contact forms or booking consultations.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, time spent, and general location data gathered automatically via cookies and analytics.
  • Communication Records: Correspondence, project requirements, and feedback exchanged via email, phone, or messaging platforms.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to your inquiries and delivering customized web design proposals.
  • Executing client contracts, web development projects, and ongoing site maintenance.
  • Improving site functionality, user experience, and overall performance.
  • Sending essential project updates, administrative notices, and invoicing details.

4. Sharing & Disclosure of Data

We respect your privacy and never sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose data only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors (such as cloud hosting providers, analytics services, or payment processors) who assist us in operating our business under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by applicable laws, court orders, or governmental regulations.

5. Data Security & Protection

We implement robust industry-standard technical and organizational security measures—including SSL/TLS encryption, secure servers, and restricted internal access—to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request the deletion of your personal data when no longer required for legitimate business purposes.
