Cookie Policy

Understanding how cookies and web technologies are used to optimize your experience on our website.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, smartphone, or tablet when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide analytical data to website owners.

2. How We Use Cookies

HC Agency uses cookies to ensure our website functions smoothly, delivers fast page load speeds, and provides a seamless browsing experience. We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for fundamental site navigation, secure contact forms, and security features.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site, which pages are most popular, and where we can optimize performance.
  • Functionality Cookies: Remember your choices (such as preferred theme or display settings) to enhance personalized navigation.

3. Third-Party Services

We may integrate privacy-focused third-party tools—such as Google Fonts for typography performance or privacy-centric web analytics—which may set cookies or collect technical metadata. These services operate under their respective privacy and cookie policies.

4. Managing & Disabling Cookies

You have full control over how your web browser handles cookies. Most modern browsers allow you to decline or remove cookies through their settings menu. Please note that restricting essential cookies may impact certain interactive features on our website.

To learn more about managing cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome Cookie Settings
  • Mozilla Firefox Cookie Settings
  • Apple Safari Cookie Settings
  • Microsoft Edge Cookie Settings

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in web standards or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
